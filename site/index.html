Click a line. Hear it.

Every line gets a timestamp. Click one to jump to that exact moment, then copy the text with or without times, or save it as a file.

Or just talk.

Press the mic to record a new note. Pause, resume and preview it, then send it to be transcribed like any other file.

Fast or Accurate. Your call.

Fast suits most voice notes. Accurate is better with heavy accents and background noise. You can also add your own models from Hugging Face.

Nothing leaves
your computer.

Other apps send your recordings to a server. VScrybe runs OpenAI's open Whisper models on your own machine, so your conversations stay yours.

  • No uploads, no account

    Audio and transcripts are processed and stored on this computer. There is nothing to sign up for.

  • Works offline

    After a one-time model download of about 480 MB, it transcribes with no internet at all.

  • Your files stay put

    Originals are never changed or moved. Delete a session and its copies go with it.

Questions, answered.

Something else? Use About → Report an issue in the app, or open an issue on GitHub.

Is it really free?

Yes. VScrybe is free and open source under the MIT licence. No trial, no account, no ads.

Does it upload my audio?

Never. Transcription runs entirely on your computer, and your recordings and transcripts are stored there too.

Why does it download 480 MB the first time?

That's the speech model that does the transcribing. It's downloaded once and then works offline. The optional Accurate model is about 1.6 GB.

Which languages does it understand?

English by default, plus French, Spanish, Portuguese, German, Italian, Dutch, Arabic, Swahili, Yoruba, Hausa, Hindi, Russian, Turkish, Chinese and Japanese, or Auto-detect.

Which files can I add?

Voice notes and recordings in .opus, .ogg, .m4a, .mp3, .wav, .amr, .aac and .webm, including WhatsApp and Telegram voice notes.
